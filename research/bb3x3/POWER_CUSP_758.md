## Outward gates at multiplicative offsets

An odd seed $P+d$ takes $J^{292}$ to $(a_0,c_0)=(2P-1,d+585)$. Compute the proposed $k$ bulk moves from $c_0$ alone by $q_i=\lfloor(c_i+1)/4\rfloor$, $c_{i+1}=6q_i+5$, $D_0=0$, $D_{i+1}=D_i+4q_i+3$. For odd $k$ the exact condition for the entire word $L^kR$ is

$$D_k\le 2P-1\le D_k+c_k.$$

The first inequality makes every bulk legal since the left decrement at each step equals its guard threshold. The last bulk leaves an even left counter, and the second inequality gives its return. An earlier left counter of one would prevent the next bulk. Both $D_k$ and $D_k+c_k$ increase weakly with $d$, so two bisections give the first admissible even offset $l_k$ and the first subsequent inadmissible offset $h_k$. The neighboring even seeds also enjoy their fixed short word. Each seed in $[P+l_k,P+h_k)$ thus advances by at most $G_k=\max(620,\lfloor(4m+9k+5)/2\rfloor)$. Every such start eventually visits the short strip $[P+h_k,P+h_k+G_k)$. Running its possible exits forward in increasing order tests whether they all merge at a single reset. The [exact table](power_ladder_758.tsv) reports $k,l_k,h_k,G_k$, the checkpoint's offset **above the edge** $P+h_k$, and the number of distinct first returns in this merger. [Reconstruct one row with `power_ladder_758.py --k 201`, or the table with `--all`](power_ladder_758.py)

All 381 successive odd values $k=13,15,\ldots,773$ yield one common checkpoint per window. Different windows can lead to the same checkpoint near the power. These gates cover long intervals at systematically spaced distances from $P$. Indeed $c_k=(3/2)^k(c_0+O(1))$ with an absolute implied constant, since each bulk differs from multiplication by $3/2$ by at most $13/2$. Also $D_k=2(c_k-c_0)-g_k$ with $5k\le g_k\le9k$. In the regime $m+k\ll d\ll P$, the edges are asymptotic to $2P/(3(3/2)^k-2)$ and $P/((3/2)^k-1)$. Successive odd values of $k$ therefore space the starts by a factor approaching $9/4$ and each window spans a factor approaching $3/2$. The stretches between windows still need connections. The checkpoint in each table row is valid independently of how the trajectory entered that row's interval.

## Enclosing a chain for a cost governed by its distance from the power

Let $P=3^m$, $m\ge20$, and $D\le d\le2D$ with $2D+2m\le P/1000$. All even seeds have the short word from the power-boundary calculation. For odd seeds choose the largest $q$ with $(9/4)^q(2D+2m+14)\le P/4$. Their launch right counter is $c_0=d+2m+1$. Since $c_i+13\le(3/2)^i(c_0+13)$, one has $c_i\le P/4$ throughout the proposed first $2q$ bulks. The total proposed left decrement is $2(c_i-c_0)-g_i$, where $g_i$ is the positive mass gain. The final left coordinate thus exceeds $3P/2-1$, and all preceding bulk guards hold. This supplies a common prefix of $q$ ordinary pairs. By maximality,

$$(4/9)^q < 9(2D+2m+14)/P.$$

Start the [ordinary enclosure tree](ANNULAR_SKIP_758.md) after these pairs. For a subsequent word with $j$ jumps and $p$ bulk pairs, its total inverse contraction, excluding the launch, is $(4/9)^qR$ with $R=3^{-j}(4/9)^p$. The [exact inverse-width inequality](RESET_WORDS_758.md) bounds its seed width by $6+(9/5)U(4/9)^qR$, with $U=O(P+m+j+p)$ bounding the mass coordinate. Stop each branch when $R\le 1/(D+m)$. Its depth is $O(\log(D+m))$, so $U=O(P)$ and its seed width is bounded by an absolute constant. Every early exceptional equality or visit to left counter one costs at most two seeds by the same ordered-counter proof. For $\delta$ satisfying $3^{-\delta}+(4/9)^\delta=1$, the two children's $R^\delta$ sum to their parent's. All stopping values exceed $1/(3(D+m))$. The tree thus has $O((D+m)^\delta)$ leaves and nodes, with $\delta\approx0.731774$. This bounds both the number of cells and the number of hard first-return queries for an enclosure of all possible live exits on this shell. Exceptional queries can still run for a long time or encounter a halt. Building the partition and widening exits incur additional factors polynomial in $\log P$; the maximum seed gain on an easy cell is $O(\log P)$.

For $m=292$, eight blocks beyond the common prefix leave 16.6996%, 16.2116%, and 14.1044% of odd positions unresolved on the shells starting at $D=2\cdot10^6$, $2\cdot10^{12}$, and $10^{80}$, respectively. The corresponding common prefixes have 750, 716, and 330 individual bulk calls. These are counts of seed positions. [Run the shell verifier](power_cusp_758.py)
