# Line 17 · an integer register for marked trits

**Machine** `1RB2RA3LB---2LB_2LA0LA4RB0RB1LA`. From its actual blank tape the machine executes more than **10³⁶ defined elementary steps before first visiting coordinate 12**. The visit itself is known to occur. The new calculation establishes a lower bound on when it occurs, and gives an integer recurrence for the marks that determine the exceptional return. Its eventual halting status remains open. The overflow-counter interpretation is due to Racheline. [Public analysis](https://wiki.bbchallenge.org/wiki/1RB2RA3LB---2LB_2LA0LA4RB0RB1LA)

Run `python3 marked_register.py` in this directory with assertions enabled. The program has no external dependencies. It checks the blank entry by running 2,308,940 elementary steps, checks the arithmetic against the individual trit rules on short words, and performs the finite integer calculation described below. Its output is in [marked-verification.txt](marked-verification.txt). The mapping from elementary instructions to header and digit operations, including safety inside each operation, is in [frontier-background.md](frontier-background.md).

## Times of marked slots

At the checkpoint the head scans a blank in A immediately before the header `231302`. There are 424 data pairs and a terminal singleton. In each completed ordinary epoch a pile of copies of `12` between the header and the data grows by one. It relays the same four slots `0100` to the first data pair. Times and emitted slots at each cut are numbered from **one**. Cut i is immediately before data pair i, numbered from zero. The physical pair `02` has trit 1 and a clear outgoing flag. All pairs 6 through 402 inclusive have this form.

Write v for the initial trit of a digit with no pending input. A plain unmarked run of length n emits exactly ⌊(n+v)/3⌋ zeros. When a mark arrives at time t with current trit r, the burst occupies slots t through t+r and ends at trit 2. It emits 2r+1 slots. For r>0 its sole mark is at position 2r in that output. For r=0 there is no child mark. Consecutive input marks are spaced at least three slots, so each burst may be completed virtually when computing mark times.

Give the digit an integer coefficient c, initially v−1. Just after a burst, if L is the last consumed slot and B the number emitted, set c=3B+1−L. At the next mark the calculation is

```text
r = (t+c) mod 3
child time = (t+c+5r)/3 if r is 1 or 2
new c = c+4(1+r) for all three residues
```

Indeed there are t−L−1 intervening plain slots starting from trit 2, or t−1 starting from v for the first mark. The number emitted before this burst is (t+c−r)/3. Add 2r for its mark. Adding 2r+1 for the entire burst and substituting the new L=t+r gives the coefficient update.

For w successive digits set C=Σ cⱼ3ʲ, numbered from the input end. At a mark set x=t+C and pass along the residues r=x mod 3. At each visited digit add 4(1+r)3ʲ to the change in C. Stop on r=0. Otherwise replace x by (x+5r)/3 and continue. At width w the final x is the absolute output mark time. This follows by division of the still-unused coefficients by 3 at each digit. Use the coefficients from before this mark for all residue tests, then add the combined change to C.

The first six trits are 2,1,0,2,0,2. Their initial coefficient sum is 181. Digit 2 has an initial pending input with trit 0. It acts as a mark on its first unmarked slot, emits one zero, and adds 4 to its coefficient. The incoming stream there starts with unmarked slots (also checked by the literal transfer), and no child mark is generated by this pending input. Thus the effective C at cut zero for the first 403 digits is 181+4·3²=217. The first mark is at time 2, so the autonomous input recurrence starts at x=219 and uses gap 4 between all consecutive input marks.

## Removing the unused high digits

For a nonnegative integer x apply f(x)=(x+5r)/3 on r=x mod 3 when r≠0. Stop on r=0 or on x=5. Above 5 the value decreases strictly. Among the remaining values, 1 leads to 2, then to 4, then to 3 where the residue is zero. Zero stops, and 5 repeats forever. Equivalently the successive residues are the low ternary digits of the 3-adic integer −x/5. Infinite passage occurs exactly when x=5(1+y) with y≥0 and every ordinary ternary digit of y in {0,1}. In fact an infinite passage reaches 5, and pulling back a step preserves divisibility by 5. For x=5(1+y), a final ternary digit 2 of y gives residue zero. Either remaining digit replaces y by ⌊y/3⌋ in this representation.

Define a signed integer D(x) by D(5)=−6, D(x)=4 for a zero residue, and D(x)=4(1+r)+3D(f(x)) otherwise. The terminal value solves −6=12+3(−6). On an infinite passage D(x)=−2−4x/5. This holds at 5 and is preserved by one substitution. For any chosen finite width w the two calculations agree as follows.

* On a mark extinguished within w digits, the finite coefficient change equals D(x).
* If 5 is reached within w digits, the finite change equals D(x)+6·3ʷ. Indeed the remaining geometric sum is 6(3ʰ−1) for h digits. The output time is 5 when the finite and infinite coefficients agree before this input.
* After q such full passages from an initially homogeneous block, the finite C exceeds the signed infinite coefficient by 6q·3ʷ. This difference leaves every one of the w residue tests unchanged and adds 6q to the next surviving output time. Consequently the surviving times are 5,11,17,… as long as every event examined satisfies one of the two depth tests.

The depth qualification matters. For any finite width, a mark can pass all of it before either a zero or 5 is encountered. The verifier tests the qualification for the entire time interval below.

## Periods and a bound on ordinary slots

Through the first six digits explicit finite-state transfer of `(0100)` repeated gives a pure period of 28 slots with marks at 3,7,14,23. Ordinary slots also matter. For N=10¹⁸ header epochs, counting emissions while skipping equal digit states at period boundaries gives exactly 777,777,777,777,777,776 slots at cut 6.

Here is the period transfer at a homogeneous digit. Suppose the incoming marks have period P. Process whole copies with the one-digit coefficient rule until hP+c is a multiple of 3. Then subsequent bursts repeat the same residues, with all child times shifted by P′=(hP+c)/3. This gives a pure outgoing period. Its first mark lies at a positive time, and its last mark is at most P′ by the timestamp formula. To see that h≤3, track the residue at the first mark of each copy. Across an inter-mark gap g it changes by r ↦ 1+g−r modulo 3. A full period therefore acts by an affine permutation of the three residues; each orbit has length at most three. Returning to the first residue is exactly the stated divisibility condition. This argument does not require P′ to be the least period.

With n inputs, include every mark at time ≤n in the coefficient, virtually finishing the last burst if necessary. The number of slots actually emitted is at most ⌊(n+c+1)/3⌋. After a completed burst this is exact by counting ordinary slots from trit 2. If the burst straddles n, its end is at most two slots beyond n, and the displayed bound includes its entire output. Before the first burst the initial c gives the exact plain count. Coefficients in a finite digit only increase on marks, so an upper bound on n also suffices. Full groups of h periods have the same coefficient gain and can be skipped; only the residual part needs inspection.

At cut 59 this calculation gives at most 33,577,048 slots of a true period of length 3,146,280 containing 393,280 marks. Finish this upper prefix with zeros to calculate the descendants of all its marks, including marks from a burst at its end. Causality ensures that the actual finite output of every layer is a prefix of this extended calculation. The finite coefficient in each layer includes every mark actually consumed there. Composing the one-digit length bounds for w=344 gives

```text
number emitted at cut 403 ≤ floor((n+C+(3^w−1)/2)/3^w).
```

Here n is the cut-59 bound and C is the finite packed coefficient after these descendants. The additive term is 1+3+⋯+3ʷ⁻¹. The integer calculation tests 4,196,890 input marks. Exactly four reach 5, and all events reach 5 or a zero residue within 36 digits. The signed coefficient ends at 1,269,218,094,854,420,032. Substituting C=1,269,218,094,854,420,032+24·3³⁴⁴ gives at most 24 output slots. The genuine output is therefore a prefix of the 24-slot word marked only at 5,11,17,23. Explicit transfer of that whole upper word through digits 403 through 408 gives output lengths 23,18,13,5,1,0.

In particular no slot reaches the terminal during these N proposed epochs. The separate initial-pending and mark-separation checks for this exact register exclude every internal A3 conflict. Induction on the finite call trees thus realizes all N epochs by the elementary machine. In epoch k, counted from zero, the head starts left of a six-cell header and k copies of `12`, visits the first data digit, and finishes two cells to the left of its starting point. Even the weaker bound of 4k+2 moves per epoch sums to 2N². Including the initial checkpoint, the first visit to 12 is later than 10³⁶ elementary steps.

## Next mathematical obstruction

The return after the eventual visit to 12 depends on the suspended register. Several admissible continuations agree with a long known prefix and produce different returns, including a halt. The genuine fifth and later mark times near cut 403 require more information about the specific periodic orbit. The scalar formulation provides an exact test for candidate fast-forward methods. A proof that an arbitrary extension can attain a branch does not identify the branch on the blank tape.
