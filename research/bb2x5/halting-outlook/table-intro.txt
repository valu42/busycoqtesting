No open machine has a calibrated estimate in raw instructions.

**Notation.** Start in A on an all-zero, two-way tape. A raw step executes one defined instruction. Reaching `---` halts. `A1` means A scanning 1. A floor measures survival from blank. `≈` abbreviates a saved exact clock. **I** marks inherited repository evidence, including the earlier outlook. **N** marks results first obtained during this pass. **E** marks an external report. “Uncalibrated” means the evidence supplies no numerical stopping-time distribution. The reports define machine-specific counters and events.

**Outlook labels.** **Proven halting** and **Proven nonhalting** require a reviewable blank-tape argument. The report states its formal verification status. **Probable halting** and **Probable nonhalting** require explicit heuristics favoring the indicated outcome. **Possible halting** identifies a specific candidate mechanism whose reachability or frequency remains open. **Unknown** means the reviewed evidence supports no direction.
