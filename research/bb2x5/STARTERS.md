## Simulation, verification and reusable tools

Use the machine and obstruction to set the scope. `SIM-<line>-<target>` can track a continuation to a named event such as the next real exception on 7. `ENGINE-<line>` can track an engine change that makes a specific machine’s deciding event observable, for example comparing a literal replay of a line 20 B2 propagation with the guarded worker. Retain the state, clock and startup provenance so a successor can derive the rule. [Resume recipes](frontiers/README.md) · [Block engine](ACCELERATORS.md)

On line 4, `FORMAL-04` formalizes a tape rewrite needed to connect a target-orbit argument to the literal blank machine. The solved line 36 is a source of independently reusable lemmas. Its historical IDs `36-BLANK`, `36-REPLAY`, `FORMAL-36-LEFT` and `FORMAL-36-RIGHT` identify existing proof and replay work. A new formal claim should specify which open machine it helps decide, for example which guard-safe conclusion on line 35 requires a new analogue of the left certificate. [Line 4 physical rules](line4.md) · [Line 36 theorem and replay](line36-rocq.md) · [Left certificate](line36-local-cut.md) · [Right certificate](line36-cap-blocks.md)
